Jingtao Sun (孙敬陶)

Postdoctoral/Research Fellow, NUS

I am currently a Research Fellow in Show Lab at the Electrical and Computer Engineering, National University of Singapore (NUS), working with Prof. Mike Shou Zheng. Prior to this, I spent a wonderful year as a Research Fellow at Social Robotics Lab (NUS), working with Prof. Sam Ge Shuzhi (IEEE Fellow).

I received my Ph.D. degree from Hunan University in 2024, where I was affiliated with the National Engineering Research Centre for Robot Visual Perception and Control (机器人视觉感知与控制技术国家工程研究中心), under the supervision of Prof. Yaonan Wang (王耀南). I also received my bachelor’s and master’s degrees from Hunan University.

My current research interests include robotics, physical AI, multimodal learning, autonomous driving.
